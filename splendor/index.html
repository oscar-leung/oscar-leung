How to play

On your turn, take one of these actions:

  • Take 3 different gem tokens.
  • Take 2 same-color tokens (only if that pile has 4+).
  • Reserve a face-up card (or the top of a tier deck) and gain 1 gold wildcard if available. Max 3 reserved at a time.
  • Buy a face-up or reserved card by paying its cost. Card bonuses you own reduce cost 1:1.

If you finish your turn with 10+ tokens, discard down to 10.

Nobles visit automatically when you own the bonuses they require (worth 3 prestige).

First to 15 prestige triggers the final round; highest total wins (ties broken by fewest cards bought).
